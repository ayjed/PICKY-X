Privacy Policy

App: Picky X (com.meshproject.pickyx) • Developer: Mesh Project

🔒 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Summary: Picky X is a local-only, single-player card matching game. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data or device identifiers. No network connection or account is required.

1. Information Collection and Use

Picky X does not collect any personal information. All game activity occurs entirely in memory and locally on your device.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track user gameplay habits, sessions, crash data, or IP addresses.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: There are no third-party libraries, ad networks, or tracking frameworks included in the app.

2. Local Device Storage

The app saves only two minimal preferences strictly on your local device using standard system storage:

  • High Scores: Lowest move count and fastest completion time.
  • Sound Preference: Sound On / Sound Off setting.

This data never leaves your device. You can reset this data at any time by clearing the app data in your Android settings or uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions

Picky X requires zero sensitive or runtime permissions. It does not request access to the internet, camera, location, contacts, microphone, or storage files.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because Picky X collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR. The app is safe for users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, the updated terms will be reflected on this page and in future versions of the app.
