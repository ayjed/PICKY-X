### 📖 Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
```text
Welcome to Picky X — the clean, offline memory card matching game designed for quick, satisfying brain training sessions anywhere, anytime!

Flip the cards, memorize their positions, and pair up all 8 matching 3D symbols in the fewest moves and fastest time possible.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• 🧠 Classic 4×4 Memory Grid: 16 cards with 8 unique pairs to challenge your focus and recall.
• ⚡ Fast & Fluid Gameplay: 3D perspective flip animations and instant card touch response.
• 🏆 High Score Tracker: Automatically saves your personal best moves and record completion times locally.
• 🔒 100% Offline & Private: No internet required, no sign-up, and zero personal data collected.
• 🚫 Zero Ads & Zero Interruptions: Pure gameplay without popups, banners, or video ads.
• 🎵 Satisfying Audio & Haptics: Crisp audio feedback and vibration with an instant one-tap sound toggle.
• 🎨 Modern Dark Theme: High-contrast graphics and smooth glowing visual effects that are easy on the eyes.

HOW TO PLAY:
1. Tap any face-down card to reveal its symbol.
2. Tap a second card to find its matching pair.
3. If the symbols match, they stay revealed!
4. If they differ, remember their locations as they flip back down.
5. Match all 8 pairs to win and set a new personal best record!

Whether you have 2 minutes to spare on your commute or want a relaxing mental workout before bed, Picky X offers a distraction-free casual card matching experience.

Download Picky X today and challenge your memory!
```
