# Privacy Policy for Picky X

**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

Thank you for playing **Picky X** ("the App"), developed by **Mesh Project**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

---

## 1. Summary (Zero Data Collection)
**Picky X is a 100% offline, local-only single-player game.**  
- We do **NOT** collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
- We do **NOT** require user accounts or registration.
- We do **NOT** track your location, IP address, or device identifiers.
- We do **NOT** include third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.
- The App requires **zero system permissions** to operate (no internet, camera, microphone, or storage permissions).

---

## 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device
The App only saves minimal gameplay preferences directly on your local device storage using standard operating system features:
- **Best Scores:** Lowest number of moves and fastest completion time.
- **Game Settings:** Your sound preference (Sound On / Sound Off).

This information remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded, shared, or accessible by us or any third party. You can reset or delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the App.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
Picky X does not integrate any third-party SDKs, frameworks, or APIs that collect data. There are no advertising partners, social media integrations, or behavioral tracking tools embedded within the App.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Because Picky X does not collect or transmit any personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play’s Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions).

---

## 5. Permissions
Picky X requests **no runtime or sensitive permissions**. The App does not access your internet connection, device contacts, location, media files, or hardware sensors.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App is strictly offline and does not connect to our servers, any updates to this policy will be posted on this page and updated in future app releases.

---
